# Prologue

# Chapter 1 – Caw’s Slingshot

As Ersatzica held the slingshot, a thought crossed her mind. It was a regular wooden slingshot with a leather band, originally treated with proper varnish to make it last, but has since seen some better days. For one, the leather band was snapped in too.

“Pointy rock,” Wryonin, her brother, had said, shrugging and entrusting the slingshot into Ersatzica’s care.

Repairing the band was near impossible, but replacing the band—that could be done. But the thought of doing so gave Ersatzica inspiration to do something extra.

Ersatzica, a teenage wolf girl, stood in the middle of her workshop, where she brings her ideas to life and brings out the best version of objects that others bring her.

This slingshot in particular was very special to her, because it was special to someone she loved: her brother.

On a giant sheet of paper, she unwound the knots of the band as she detached it from the slingshot, meticulously drawing the curves as she did so. She laid the untied band flat on the paper.

Then she reached up into the cupboards and pulled out a leather sheet. She flipped her trusty screwdriver to the leather knife side and cut a strip of leather off the sheet, matching the snapped band as closely as possible.

Lastly, she tied the band onto the slingshot, matching the knots that were previously there.

Admiring her handy work, a thought crossed her mind.

See, Wryonin uses his slingshot to hunt, and he’s quite good at it. And when he goes hunting, he hunts with his raven. Or well, he used to. It’s been awhile since his raven died. Wryonin hasn’t really been the same since.

And it’s really faint, but if you look closely at the slingshot, at the right angle, you can see a flash of blue light glimmer off the slingshot, as if the sun is reflecting off it at the perfect angle. But you can see this even in the dark.

It has a certain energy to it, that’s hard to describe. But it *feels* like the same energy as his raven had. Like his raven is in there, calling to him, saying it’s going to be ok.

So, the thought. There’s no decoration on the slingshot. There’s no indication on the outside that his raven is still with him through this item. And I don’t think he’s yet aware that Alpha has given him this gift of grief. So it might make him happy to see that He has.

Ersatzica took out a can of black paint and a brush. She etched a quick sketch of a raven’s silhouette. Then she inked the sketch with the brush. She set the slingshot on the table to dry.

The faint blue shimmer still shone behind the paint, this time a little brighter despite being partially covered.

“You’re welcome.” Ersatzica beamed as she stroked the side of the slingshot, in much the same way that Wryonin used to stroke his raven’s head.
