Ersatzica Wolf Chiefling

# Chapter 1 – Drums of Retriyoan

Problem: Ersatzica wants to saw off spike from Wryonin’s slingshot

Effect: Wryonin yells at her and stops talking to her

Catalyst: Tigrin’s attack on Ersie

Resolution: Wryonin realizes that Ersatzica did it to help him, and that the change actually helps him, through repeated use without injury

Resolution: Ersatzica learns that she should get permission to fundamentally change an item of someone else’s, even if it would benefit them. She learns this bc mudrich takes her screwdriver and gets mud on it, and ersatzica has a hard time getting the mud out of the file part, making it hard to use, but also easier to grasp. She also realizes how people sometimes live with inconvenient aspects of their tools without realizing how it hurts them.

Resolution: Wryonin and Ersatzica talk it out after the battle with Tigrin, and make up.

Also:

-   the 4 siblings wait for Uncle Yotleph, but he never shows up.
-   Ersie works on the Drums of Retriyoan
-   Fisher saves Ersie

Cliffhanger: Muddy is injured

Resolution: Ersie tells Muddie she has her own work to do, and actually does that first

Resolution: Muddie follows Ersie, but respects her decision

Also:

-   They discover the Cuffs of Tigrin increase strength
-   Muddy takes them

Cliffhanger: Muddy punches hole in wall in Ersie’s workshop

# Chapter 3 – Boots of Pantrinome

Inciting incident: Ersie and Muddie discover drums and boots in the hole in the workshop wall

Problem: Ersie and Muddie disagree about what to do with the drums and boots. They both recognize the items as those worn by “Pantrinome the Assassin Cat”. Ersie wants to fix them up, but Muddie wants to destroy them.

Resolution: Ersie convinces Muddy that if the boots and drums are evil, then her cuffs are too, so Muddy agrees to let Ersie fix them up, as long as Ersie agrees to help her with her tunnels for a week.

Also:

-   Ersie fixes up the boots
-   Ersie gets Nosalen’s Foot

Cliffhanger: Ersie tries on the boots, turns into a cat

# Chapter 4 – Nosalen’s Foot

Problem: Ersie has to run from her family, runs to the cat side

Problem: If the cats are evil, how can she trust them?

Problem: Who can she trust to help her with this problem?

Resolution: She can’t trust a random guard, cant trust Nephalie, cant even trust Mudrich, maybe cats are the only ones she can trust

Resolution: She accepts that she’s now evil, and she runs to the cat side

Also:
